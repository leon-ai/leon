# Leon 2.0

Leon is your open-source personal AI assistant.

It is built around tools, memory, context, and owner control. The goal is not to make another chatbot. The goal is to give people their own assistant, one they can run, inspect, extend, and shape around their real life and real machine.

Leon has been in the works since 2017. The first beta was released in February 2019. The mission has stayed clear: personal AI should feel personal, useful, and owned by the person using it.
